Buyer questions

Before you send an offer.

Does the app need a server I have to run?

No. Each salon's data lives in an encrypted database on its phone. Backup, branches and online booking use one Google Apps Script that you deploy once in your own Google account, at no hosting cost.

How do I get paid by salons?

The activation screen sends the owner to your checkout link (Stripe, Razorpay, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy or any page) with plan, billing period, currency, country and device ID. You issue a signed key from the command line or in batches for a Google Sheet. In India, owners can also pay by UPI from the phone.

What traction does it have?

The founder shares current installs, customers and any revenue on a call, with screenshots from the licence sheet and Play Console. The price on this page is for the finished product, brand and system.

Is it ready for the Play Store and the App Store?

Android builds (AAB for Play, APK for direct sale) are scripted with EAS. An iOS build profile is included; publishing on iOS needs your Apple developer account and a test pass on iPhone.

Can I rebrand it?

Yes. Name, colours and logo come from one theme file and one brand folder; a script regenerates every icon and splash image from a new logo.

How does the transfer work?

Funds go into escrow. The founder then transfers the code repository, the domain, the licence signing keys and the brand files, and runs the handover calls. Escrow releases when you confirm you have everything.
